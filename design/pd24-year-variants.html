Tap any month (a row in A, a month label in B, a card in C) to open it; tap a day inside to see its card. A day mark in the canvas is 9–13 pt — under the 28 pt control minimum — so the month is the tap target and the day is reached one level in, the way iOS Calendar goes year → month.
