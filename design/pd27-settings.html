The screen is live. “Create key” shows a freshly generated key (state 2); “Key saved” moves to state 3. “I already have a key” opens the entry field (state 4) — it normalises case, spaces and dashes as you type; a key that is long enough enables “Restore”, and a key of all zeros (0000-0000-0000-0000-0000-0000) lands on the wrong-key state on purpose. Appearance, Text size, Motion, Keyboard, Presentation and Safe area are review switches only: the product follows the system settings and ships no appearance control (dark-mode.md > Best practices). The grey slab stands in for the system keyboard at an APPROXIMATE iPhone 16 height — the real height has to be checked on device.
