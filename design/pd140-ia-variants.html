Four ways to arrange the same product. Light and dark side by side, 390×844, simulated safe areas 50 / 34 pt. Tap counts, sources and the verdict live in research/usability-2026-10/ia-review.md. Reading the four at once

A and D keep the daily ritual at one tap; B charges two taps a day forever to buy a map of the product that is read once. C keeps A’s daily path and gives the second tab the things it is missing anyway (Continue, clue counts, a Mode row, a second door to the archive), which is why the review recommends it. The full argument, tap counts per scenario and the sources behind the conventions are in research/usability-2026-10/ia-review.md.
